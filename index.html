📍 Destination

Jamui Heritage & Nature

Explore ancient Gidhaur palace archives, Simultala pine valley retreats, Patneshwar stone artisans, and eco-guides.

📜 Why Visit & History

Ancient seat of the Gidhaur royal dynasty with the iconic 19th-century Minto Tower, serene pine-clad hills of Simultala ("Mini Shimla of Bihar"), sacred Patneshwar temple, and rich Nagi-Nakti bird sanctuary.

🚂 Getting There
Distance from Patna: ~160 km
Nearest Rail Station: Jamui (JMU) / Jhajha (JAJ)
Approx Travel Time: ~3.5 Hours
💡 Visitor Tips
Best Season: Oct to March
Tip: Taste fresh Gidhaur chhena sweets early morning and hire a certified local guide.
