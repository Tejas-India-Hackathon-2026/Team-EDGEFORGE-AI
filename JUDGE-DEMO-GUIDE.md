### 0:00–0:35 — Problem and promise

Say:

> Bihar has world-class heritage, food and crafts, but discovery is fragmented and small local providers remain hard to find. GhoomoBihar gives a visitor one trusted discovery flow and connects them directly to local providers without a middleman commission.

Show the destination grid and mention the five pilot footprints: Jamui, Gaya, Nalanda, Saran and Patna.

### 0:35–1:20 — Topic-specific marketplace listings

1. Open one destination.
2. Filter between guide, homestay, food and craft.
3. Point out that each listing image matches its subject.
4. Show price, hours, verification status, phone and WhatsApp actions.

Do not claim the displayed phone numbers, prices or badges are audited production records. They are prototype demonstration data.

### 1:20–2:30 — Plan My Bihar Yatra

1. Open **Ask Shartak**.
2. Choose **Plan My Bihar Yatra**.
3. Select duration, daily budget, preferred experience and starting location.
4. Show the day-wise itinerary, travel-time estimates, indicative budget and local listing recommendations.
5. Point out the direct WhatsApp action.

Say:

> This guided planner is deterministic and grounded in our curated Bihar dataset, so it remains available even when an external AI provider is unavailable.

### 2:30–3:10 — Direct-to-Local Impact proof

Open an itinerary or booking confirmation and show:

- `100% direct vendor contact — no middleman commission`;
- supported provider types;
- verification or pending-verification status;
- estimated local livelihood impact.

Say:

> The amount is explicitly labelled as an estimated demo metric. It represents potential direct local spend calculated from displayed indicative prices—not confirmed payment, vendor profit or audited income.

### 3:10–4:00 — Vendor and admin workflow

1. Open the three-dot account menu.
2. Show the vendor registration/listing flow.
3. Explain that vendor identity uses Supabase Auth.
4. Show the admin review concept only if the secure admin session is already working.

Do not spend presentation time troubleshooting authentication. If login fails, explain the architecture from the README and continue.

### 4:00–4:35 — Feedback-driven expansion

1. Show the tourism feedback form.
2. Show the district roadmap.

### 4:35–5:00 — Close

Say:

> GhoomoBihar is not only a travel directory. It is an inclusive tourism marketplace prototype that combines discovery, direct local connection, practical itinerary planning, transparent impact estimates and a responsible path to all 38 Bihar districts.

## Likely judge questions

### Is every Shartak response generated by live AI?

No. The interface clearly reports `Live AI Guide` when the server returns an upstream response and `Local AI Guide` when it uses the curated browser fallback. The Yatra planner is deterministic and works independently of the external AI provider.

### Are vendor listings centrally synchronized?

Not yet. Listing and inquiry persistence is browser-local in the prototype. Supabase already handles vendor identity and tourism feedback; centralized marketplace tables are the first production-hardening milestone.

### What does “verified” mean?

It is a prototype moderation status, not a government certification. Production verification would require document checks, identity validation, local moderation ownership and periodic re-verification.

### Does the platform take a commission?

The prototype processes no payment and charges no middleman commission. Travelers contact providers directly. Future monetization should use transparent provider tools, onboarding or institutional partnerships without hiding traveler-facing fees.

### Is the livelihood-impact number real income?

No. It is an estimated demo metric derived from displayed indicative prices. It is not a completed transaction, vendor profit or audited livelihood income.

### How will the product scale to 38 districts?

Through district entry gates: verified provider supply, official-source content review, transport and safety guidance, a named local moderation partner and feedback-based demand signals. See [DISTRICT-EXPANSION-ROADMAP.md](DISTRICT-EXPANSION-ROADMAP.md).

### How are secrets protected?

Admin credential hashes, the admin session-signing secret and Groq API key live only in Netlify environment variables. Admin authentication happens in a Netlify Function and returns a signed HttpOnly cookie. No plaintext admin credential is committed or stored in browser localStorage.
